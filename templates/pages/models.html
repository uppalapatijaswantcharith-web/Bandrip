ABOUT THE STUDIO ↓

+++ 01 / THE STUDIO

WE CAST, TRAINAND SHOOT MODELSFOR STREETWEAR.

The studio is where BANDRIP's clothes meet the people who wear them. New faces are cast, coached on walk and pose, styled in the collection and photographed for the label's lookbooks and shows. Experience is not required to apply.

+++ 02 / STYLING

WEAR IT YOUR WAY

Oversized pieces change with the person inside them. Models learn how to stack cargos over sneakers, how a drop shoulder sits, how to wear a bandana as a mask, a headband or a neck tie, and how to bring their own references into a look.

  • FIT · LAYER · PROPORTION
  • COLOUR · ACCESSORIES · ATTITUDE
