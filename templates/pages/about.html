+++ ABOUT BANDRIP

STREETUNIFORM,STUDIO MADE.

BANDRIP is a unisex streetwear label and a modelling studio under one roof. The label makes oversized everyday pieces; the studio casts and trains the people who wear them in shoots and shows.

COPY IS A DRAFT FOR CLIENT REVIEW.

CUT FOR THE STREET

Every piece starts from how it will be worn outside: room to move, fabric that holds its shape, proportions that stack. Tees are boxy, cargos are wide, shirts are long.

BUILT IN THE STUDIO

The studio is part of the brand, not a service on the side. Fits are tested on studio models, and the lookbooks are shot by the same team that styles them.

WORN BY YOU

The clothes are unisex and the casting is open. BANDRIP wants its images to look like the people who actually wear it.
